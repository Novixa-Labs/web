Privacy Policy

Magnet Yard: Logic Puzzle

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Novixa-Labs operates Magnet Yard: Logic Puzzle (package com.novixalabs.magnetyard). It is a single-player magnetic logic puzzle and does not require an account.

1. Data stored on your device

  • Unlocked levels, best star ratings, hint balance, selected skin, tutorial status, sound, haptics, and reduced-motion settings.
  • Whether Magnet Yard Plus has been purchased through Google Play.

Game progress remains on your device except where the services below are involved. We do not sell personal information.

2. Advertising and consent

The free version may show a full-screen ad only at an eligible natural break after level completion. It never shows one on launch or before play. Rewarded videos are optional and grant three hints only after the reward is earned. Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform asks for consent and provides ad privacy choices. Magnet Yard Plus removes ads.

Ads use Google AdMob mediation. Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) may participate in mediation, and Unity may provide fallback when AdMob has no fill. These providers may process advertising ID, IP address, coarse location, device and app details, consent signals, and ad interactions for delivery, measurement, security, and frequency limits. See Google Privacy Policy, Unity Privacy Policy, and Liftoff Privacy Policy.

3. Firebase

Release builds use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics records campaign attribution and limited outcomes such as level starts, the first successful magnet pulse on an install, hints used, level completion summaries, earned rewards, purchases, and actual ad displays. It does not record board contents or every touch or pulse. Crashlytics processes crash diagnostics such as app/device state and stack traces. See Firebase privacy information.

4. Purchases

Google Play processes the one-time Magnet Yard Plus purchase. We do not receive your payment-card details. Restore purchase uses the same Google account.

5. Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.
