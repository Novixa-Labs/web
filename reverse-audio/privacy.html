Privacy Policy

Reverse Audio: Singing Voice · Last updated: November 22, 2026

Novixa-Labs operates Reverse Audio: Singing Voice (package com.novixalabs.reverseaudio). This policy explains the information processed when you use the app and the choices available to you.

Audio, media, and local app data

The app records audio only after you grant the microphone permission. It can also open an audio file that you select using Android's system file picker. Recording, reversal, playback, scoring, local clip storage, and video export are performed on your device. We do not upload your recordings, imported audio, exported videos, or speech-pack contents to Novixa-Labs servers.

Saved clips, settings, progress, and optional downloaded speech-recognition packs remain on your device until you delete them in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Video exports are saved to your device's media library only when you choose to export. If you share a clip or export, the receiving app or service processes it under its own policy.

Optional offline speech packs

If you choose to download an optional speech pack, the app downloads the selected Vosk model from its provider so that word timing can run on-device. The app does not send your recordings to Vosk for recognition.

Advertising and consent

The free version may show advertising through Google AdMob, including mediation with Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize. Ad providers may process advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostics to deliver, measure, and protect advertising. The app may offer optional rewarded ads for selected features. It does not show an ad merely because the app launches.

Where required, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) requests consent before ads are requested and provides an in-app Privacy options entry when one is required. You can also manage advertising controls in Android or Google settings. A paid Pro entitlement stops ad display; it does not necessarily stop processing needed for purchases, analytics, crash reporting, or legal obligations.

Analytics and crash reports

Release builds may use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics records limited app and feature events, such as app state, successful reveals, export completion, settings changes, rewarded-ad actions, and purchase-related events. Crashlytics receives technical crash and error diagnostics. The app is designed not to send recorded or imported audio as analytics events.

Purchases

If you buy Reverse Audio Pro, Google Play Billing processes the payment. We do not receive your full payment-card number. Google Play and the app process product, purchase-state, and entitlement information to provide and restore the purchase.

Service providers

Google Play, Google AdMob, Google UMP, Firebase, Unity Ads, and Liftoff Monetize process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Useful references include Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information, Unity Privacy Policy, and Liftoff Privacy Policy.

Children and changes to this policy

The app is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy when the app, its providers, or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the current version.
