2. Tracing tips

  • Turn on Max brightness on open in Settings for the lightbox effect.
  • Use thin paper over your phone screen in a dim room.
  • Lock the art after positioning so it does not move while you draw.

3. Ads & Pro

  • Free version shows banner ads on main tabs and occasional interstitials after completing a trace.
  • Watch a rewarded video for 24-hour access to locked capybaras, or buy capy_trace_pro once to remove ads and unlock everything.
  • Use Restore purchases in Settings after reinstall.

4. Data deletion

Android Settings → Apps → Capy Trace → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall. Play purchases remain with Google.
