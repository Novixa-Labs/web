Privacy Policy

Backdrop: Video Background Remover

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Novixa-Labs operates Backdrop (package com.altatweer.backdrop). No account is required. We do not sell personal information.

On-device media processing

Videos, camera recordings, masks, edits, projects, and ordinary exports are processed and stored on your device. They are not uploaded for background removal. If you explicitly request an AI-generated background, only your text prompt and an anonymous installation identifier are sent to our service; your video frames are not sent.

Permissions

Backdrop requests video access to select clips, camera and microphone access only for Live Camera recording, notifications for long-running processing, and network access for optional AI scenes, ads, purchases, analytics, and crash reporting.

Advertising and consent

The free version may show a banner on top-level browsing screens, a full-screen ad only at a natural post-export break, and an optional rewarded video that unlocks one video export. Ads are never shown at app launch, on resume, or while editing. Google User Messaging Platform presents consent choices where required. AdMob mediation may use Google, Unity Ads, and Liftoff Monetize (Vungle), which may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device/app data, consent signals, and ad interactions. Backdrop Pro removes banner and full-screen ads.

Firebase

Release builds use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. Analytics records a small set of campaign-relevant outcomes such as opening the export sheet, earning an export reward, starting and completing an export, completed purchases, and actual ad impressions. It does not record video content, prompts, filenames, per-frame processing, every tap, or slider movement. Crashlytics receives crash diagnostics needed to improve reliability.

Purchases

Google Play processes subscriptions and lifetime purchases. We do not receive payment-card details. Purchase tokens may be sent to our server and Google Play solely to verify entitlement and grant applicable service credits.

Retention and deletion

Local projects remain until you delete them or uninstall/clear app data. Crash, analytics, advertising, and purchase records follow the retention controls of Google and the applicable ad providers. You may delete local projects in the app and can contact us about service-side data linked to an anonymous installation identifier.
