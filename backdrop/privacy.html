Privacy Policy

Backdrop: Video BG Remover

Effective and last updated: November 1, 2026

Novixa-Labs ("we," "us," or "our") operates Backdrop, package com.novixalabs.backdrop. This policy explains what information the app and its service providers process, why it is processed, and the choices available to you. No Backdrop account is required. We do not sell your personal information.

1. What the app does

Backdrop lets you select or record video, remove or replace its background, edit the result, save projects, and export media. Ordinary segmentation, compositing, refinement, project storage, and export run on your device. Source videos, audio, extracted frames, masks, filenames, and local projects are not uploaded to us for ordinary editing.

2. Permissions and local information

  • Photos and videos: used only to let you choose source media and save exports. The app may use Android's system picker or request video-library access for its custom gallery.
  • Camera and microphone: requested only when you use Live Camera to preview and record video with audio.
  • Notifications and foreground processing: used to show progress or completion for long-running processing and exports.
  • Network and device state: used for ads, consent, purchases, optional AI scenes, analytics, crash reporting, and service availability.

Projects and preferences remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Exported media remains in device storage until you remove it.

3. Optional AI-generated backgrounds

If you explicitly request an AI-generated scene, the text prompt you enter and a random per-install identifier are sent over HTTPS to our service and an image-generation provider to return the requested image and enforce service credits. Your source video, video frames, masks, filenames, contacts, and precise location are not sent with the prompt. Do not include personal or confidential information in a prompt.

4. Advertising, mediation, and consent

The free version may show banner ads on suitable browsing screens, a full-screen ad at a natural post-export break, and an optional rewarded ad that grants one export after the reward is completed. Ads are not intended to interrupt active editing, appear at app launch, or appear merely because the app resumes.

Ads are served through Google AdMob. AdMob mediation integrates Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize (formerly Vungle). Depending on region, consent, device settings, and ad availability, these providers may process the Android advertising ID, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device and app information, consent signals, diagnostic data, and ad views, clicks, or reward events to deliver, limit, secure, and measure advertising.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents privacy choices before requesting personalized advertising. You can revisit available ad privacy choices in the app, limit or reset the advertising ID in Android settings, decline an optional rewarded ad, or purchase an eligible Backdrop Pro plan to remove banner and full-screen advertising. A Pro plan does not remove network processing required for purchases or optional online features.

5. Analytics and crash diagnostics

Production releases may use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics records a limited set of product outcomes, such as export-sheet opens, rewarded-export completion, export start or completion, completed purchases, and actual ad impressions. Crashlytics may receive stack traces, device/app information, and diagnostics needed to investigate failures. We do not intentionally send video content, prompts, filenames, per-frame data, every tap, or editor slider movements to Firebase.

Google's retention and deletion controls apply to Firebase and advertising data processed by Google. We configure these services for product operation, measurement, security, and reliability.

6. In-app purchases and subscriptions

Backdrop may offer weekly and yearly subscriptions and a lifetime, one-time Pro purchase through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes payment details; we do not receive your full card number. The app receives product, purchase-state, and entitlement information needed to unlock or restore Pro. A purchase token, product identifier, and anonymous installation identifier may be sent to our server and Google Play over HTTPS to verify the purchase and grant applicable service credits.

Subscriptions renew and can be cancelled in Google Play according to the price, trial, renewal, and eligibility terms shown before purchase. Restore uses purchases available to the same Google account. Refunds are handled under Google Play policy and applicable law.

7. Data sharing

We share information only as needed with service providers described above, to provide a feature you request, comply with law, protect users or services, or complete a business transfer subject to appropriate notice and legal safeguards. Providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not rent or sell source media, prompts, or personal information.

8. Retention, deletion, and security

Local projects and settings are controlled through the app or Android system settings. Service-side records associated with an anonymous installation identifier are retained only as reasonably necessary for purchase verification, service credits, security, legal obligations, and dispute prevention. Advertising, Firebase, Google Play, Unity, and Liftoff records follow those providers' retention policies.

You may email us to request access to or deletion of service-side data associated with your installation identifier. We may ask for information necessary to locate the record and verify the request. No system is completely secure, but network requests under our control use HTTPS and we apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards.

9. Children

Backdrop is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The intended Play audience is 13 and older. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

10. International users and policy changes

Service providers may process information in countries other than yours, subject to their legal safeguards. We may update this policy when features, providers, or legal requirements change. The revised date will appear above, and material changes may also be communicated in the app or on this page.
