Privacy Policy

Deckly: PPT Editor & Presenter

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Novixa-Labs operates the Android app Deckly: PPT Editor & Presenter (package name: com.novixalabs.deckly). This policy explains what the app accesses, how data is used, and your choices.

1. Summary

  • No account is required.
  • Presentation files you open are stored and edited on your device using Storage Access Framework (SAF).
  • We do not upload your slide content to Novixa-Labs servers.
  • The free app may show ads (banner and occasional full-screen ads). Deckly Pro is a one-time purchase that removes ads.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to improve stability (anonymous usage and crash data).

2. Files & permissions

Deckly opens .pptx, .ppsx, and .potx files only when you choose them in a file picker or when another app shares a file to Deckly. We do not request broad storage permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).

Exports (PDF, images, copies) are written only to locations you choose or to MediaStore folders (Downloads / Pictures) per Android version.

3. Internet, ads & AD_ID

The app uses the internet for advertisements, in-app purchases (Google Play), and Firebase services.

Ads are served through Google AdMob with Open Bidding partners such as Unity Ads and Liftoff (Vungle). The app declares com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID where required for ad measurement. You can limit ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or purchase Deckly Pro.

4. Analytics & crashes

Firebase may collect app version, device model, OS version, coarse usage events (for example screen views, export completed), and crash stack traces. See Firebase Privacy and Google Privacy Policy.

5. In-app purchases

Deckly Pro (product ID deckly_pro) is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We receive purchase confirmation to unlock Pro on your Google account. We do not store payment card details.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
