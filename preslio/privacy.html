Privacy Policy

Preslio: PPTX Editor & Viewer

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Novixa-Labs operates the Android app Preslio: PPTX Editor & Viewer (package name: com.novixalabs.preslio). This policy explains what the app accesses, how data is used, and your choices.

1. Summary

  • No account is required.
  • Presentation files you open are stored and edited on your device using Storage Access Framework (SAF).
  • We do not upload your slide content to Novixa-Labs servers.
  • The free app may show ads (banner and occasional full-screen ads). Preslio Pro is a one-time purchase that removes ads.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to improve stability (anonymous usage and crash data).

2. Files & permissions

Preslio opens .ppt, .pptx, .ppsx, and .potx files when you choose them in a file picker or when another app shares a file to Preslio. Legacy .ppt support depends on your device and file; when a file cannot be opened, save a copy as .pptx in PowerPoint or Google Slides and open that copy in Preslio. We do not request broad storage permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).

Exports (PDF, images, copies) are written only to locations you choose or to MediaStore folders (Downloads / Pictures) per Android version.

3. Internet, ads & AD_ID

The app uses the internet for advertisements, in-app purchases (Google Play), and Firebase services.

Ads are served through Google AdMob with Open Bidding partners such as Unity Ads and Liftoff (Vungle). The app declares com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID where required for ad measurement. You can limit ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or purchase Preslio Pro.

4. Analytics & crashes

Firebase may collect app version, device model, OS version, coarse usage events (for example screen views, export completed), and crash stack traces. See Firebase Privacy and Google Privacy Policy.

5. In-app purchases

Preslio Pro (product ID preslio_pro) is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We receive purchase confirmation to unlock Pro on your Google account. We do not store payment card details.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
