Privacy Policy

Smart ATV Remote · Novixa-Labs
Last updated: July 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart ATV Remote ("the app", "we", "us"), published by Novixa-Labs, handles information when you use the app. We built Smart ATV Remote to be privacy-friendly: it works entirely on your device and your local Wi-Fi network.

The short version: We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. We have no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything the app needs stays on your phone.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no sign-up, no login, and no user accounts. We do not gather your name, email, contacts, location, or device identifiers, and we do not transmit any data to our servers (we do not operate any).

2. Data Stored On Your Device

To function as a remote, the app saves the following locally on your device only:

  • Paired TV details — the name and local network address of TVs you connect to, so you can reconnect quickly.
  • A pairing certificate/key — generated on your device to establish a secure connection with your TV, as required by the Android TV remote protocol.
  • Your preferences — layout, app shortcuts, and settings you choose.

This information never leaves your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

3. Permissions We Use & Why

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — used only when you tap the voice button to search on your TV. Speech is converted to text using your device's built-in speech recognition and the resulting text is sent to your TV. We do not record, store, or share audio.
  • Network & Wi-Fi access (Internet, Network State, Wi-Fi State, Wi-Fi Multicast) — used to discover your TV on your local Wi-Fi network and to send remote-control commands to it. The app communicates only with your TV on your own network.

The app requests no other sensitive permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, photos, files, or camera.

4. Speech Recognition

Voice search relies on the speech-recognition service built into your device's operating system (Android/iOS). Depending on your device settings, that service may process audio to produce text. This is handled by your device's platform, not by us, and is governed by your device manufacturer's and operating system's privacy policies. Smart ATV Remote only receives the recognized text so it can be typed on your TV.

5. Advertising & Purchases

The current version of Smart ATV Remote contains no advertisements and no in-app purchases or subscriptions. It does not use an advertising ID. If we introduce ads or premium features in a future version, we will update this Privacy Policy and, where required, request your consent before any additional data is processed.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any. No data is sold or transferred for advertising or analytics purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

Smart ATV Remote is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since we collect no data, the app is safe for use by all ages under adult supervision.

8. Security

Connections to your TV use the secure, certificate-based pairing defined by the Android TV remote protocol. Because the app stores data only on your device, you remain in control of it at all times.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above and, when significant, communicated within the app or store listing.
