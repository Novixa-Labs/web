2. Ads & Pro

  • The free version may show ads (AdMob, Unity fallback). Where required, a consent form (UMP) may appear.
  • Watch a rewarded video to unlock premium content for 24 hours, or buy the one-time product drowsy_pro to remove ads and unlock premium features permanently.
  • After reinstall, use Restore purchases with the same Google account.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play.

3. Camera

If the challenge cannot start, open Android Settings → Apps → Drowsy → Permissions and allow Camera.

4. Data deletion

Android Settings → Apps → Drowsy → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall. Play purchases remain with Google and can restore Pro.
