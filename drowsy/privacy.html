Privacy Policy

Drowsy: Yawn Game & Sleep

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Novixa-Labs operates the mobile application Drowsy (package name: com.novixalabs.drowsy). This policy explains what the app accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Summary

  • No account is required. We do not sell your personal information.
  • Sleep diary, game stats, and preferences stay on your device.
  • Camera frames during the yawn challenge are processed on-device and are never uploaded.
  • The free version may show ads (banner, interstitial after a successful challenge, optional rewarded video). You can watch a video to unlock premium content for 24 hours, or buy a one-time Pro purchase to remove ads and unlock premium levels, sounds, and reels permanently.
  • Where required, we show a Google UMP consent form before personalized ads.

2. What the App Does

  • A camera challenge with on-device face detection
  • Yawn reels and optional local reel recording
  • Ambient sleep sounds and wind-down sessions
  • A local sleep diary and optional bedtime reminders
  • Optional advertising and a one-time Pro purchase

3. Information Stored on Your Device

  • Sleep diary entries, game statistics, player display name, and preferences
  • Downloaded reel/sound cache and user-created reels you save
  • Bedtime reminder schedule
  • Pro / rewarded-unlock status

This data stays on your device unless you clear app data, uninstall, or share content yourself.

4. Camera & Microphone

The yawn challenge and reel recording use the camera (and microphone only while you record a reel). Processing is on-device. We do not upload camera video to our servers.

5. Network Use

The app may use the internet to:

  • Download public content catalogs and media (GitHub / jsDelivr)
  • Load advertisements (Google AdMob; Unity Ads as a no-fill fallback)
  • Process in-app purchases through Google Play
  • Show consent / privacy messaging (UMP) where required
  • Send anonymous analytics and crash reports (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics)
  • Open the Play Store listing or our website when you choose those actions

6. Advertising, Consent & Advertising ID

If you have not purchased Pro, the app may display advertisements through Google AdMob. If AdMob has no fill, Unity Ads may be used as a client-side fallback for banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads. Native ads are not shown in this app.

These services may process advertising ID, IP address, device type, OS version, ad interaction data, and approximate location derived from IP.

Where required, we use Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can also limit ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or purchase Pro to stop ads in the app.

See Google ads policy and the Unity privacy policy.

7. In-App Purchases

We offer a one-time in-app product (Pro, product ID drowsy_pro) processed by Google Play Billing. Google processes payment; we do not store your card or bank details. Use restore purchases if you reinstall on the same Google account.

8. Analytics & Crash Reporting

Drowsy uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for anonymous events and crash stacks. These services do not receive your camera feed or diary notes.

9. Notifications

If you enable bedtime reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. We may also schedule an optional idle reminder to return to the app. Notification content is not sent to our servers.

10. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Name, email, or phone for an account (unless you email us for support)
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts, SMS, call logs, or calendar

11. Children's Privacy

Drowsy is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data remains until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to respond.
  • Ad and billing providers retain data according to their policies.

To remove all app data: Android Settings → Apps → Drowsy → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

13. Not a Medical Device

Drowsy is a relaxation and entertainment app. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or sleep disorder. Do not use the app while driving or operating machinery.

14. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the "Last updated" date. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
