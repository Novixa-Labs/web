Privacy Policy

Junk Journal

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Novixa-Labs ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile application Junk Journal (package name: com.novixalabs.junkjournal). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

By installing or using the app, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Summary (Plain Language)

Junk Journal is a creative scrapbooking / collage journal app. You design pages with papers, stickers, photos, and text, then export or share them.

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Journals and pages you create are stored on your device.
  • Photos you import stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them.
  • Current version: no ads and no in-app purchases. If we enable them later, we will update this policy and Play Store disclosures first.

2. What the App Does

The app lets you:

  • Create and edit digital junk-journal / collage pages
  • Import photos via the Android system photo picker
  • Use craft packs (papers, stickers, ephemera) that may download over the internet
  • Export pages as images or PDF, save to gallery, or share via the system share sheet

3. Information We Access

A. Information stored on your device (local only)

  • Your journals, pages, and collage layouts
  • Imported photo files you choose to place on a page
  • App preferences stored locally

This data stays on your device unless you clear app data or uninstall. We do not operate a cloud sync account for journals.

B. Photos and media

When you import a photo, the app opens the Android system photo picker so you can choose a single image. We do not request broad gallery access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES).

When you save an export to the gallery, the app writes only that exported image via the system MediaStore. We do not scan your photo library in the background.

C. Internet access

The app may use the internet to download optional craft-pack assets or catalogs. We do not show advertisements in the current release.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Name, email, or phone number (unless you email us for support voluntarily)
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts, SMS, call logs, or calendar
  • Passwords or account credentials (there is no app login)

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play — app distribution
  • Content hosts / CDN (if used) — public craft-pack assets

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local journals remain until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to respond.

To remove all app data: Android Settings → Apps → Junk Journal → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures such as HTTPS for network requests. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. You control which photos you import and what you share or export.

9. Your Rights and Choices

  • Choose which photo to import via the system photo picker (or cancel)
  • Clear app data or uninstall at any time
  • Contact us with privacy questions

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version and update the "Last updated" date. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
