Privacy Policy

Let It Out: Vent & Smash · Last updated: November 26, 2026

Novixa Labs operates Let It Out: Vent & Smash (package com.novixalabs.letitout). This policy explains the information processed when you use the app.

Your private app data

Vent sessions, pressure readings, selected feeling words, settings, and shelf items are stored on your device. Text entered in a room is cleared when you leave it, except for a lesson you deliberately save to your shelf. We do not operate a server for this content and do not upload it to Novixa Labs.

Microphone

The Scream room uses the microphone only after you grant permission to measure loudness while you use the feature. Audio is processed in memory and is not recorded, saved, or sent to us.

Advertising, consent, analytics, and crashes

The free version may show advertising through Google AdMob with Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize mediation. Where required, Google User Messaging Platform requests consent before ads are requested and provides Privacy options when required. Advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device/app information, ad interactions, and diagnostics under their own policies.

Release builds may use Firebase Analytics for limited feature events such as completed vent sessions and purchase-related activity, and Firebase Crashlytics for technical crash diagnostics. The app is designed not to send your typed text, feelings, shelf lessons, or microphone audio in analytics or crash reports.

Purchases

Google Play Billing processes Pro purchases and restoration. We do not receive your full payment-card number. Google Play and the app process product, purchase-state, and entitlement information to provide the purchase.

Choices, deletion, and contact

You can delete all locally stored app data in Settings, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. You can manage ad controls through Android or Google settings. For privacy questions, email bibi85259@gmail.com; please do not send personal journal content or payment information by email.

Service providers and changes

Google Play, Google AdMob, Google UMP, Firebase, Unity Ads, and Liftoff Monetize process information under their own policies, including the Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information, Unity Privacy Policy, and Liftoff Privacy Policy. This app is not a medical device or a replacement for professional support. We may update this policy when the app or its providers change.
