Privacy Policy

Bead 12

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Novixa-Labs ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile application Bead 12 (package name: com.novixalabs.beads12). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

By installing or using the app, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Summary (Plain Language)

Bead 12 is a traditional bead board game (also known as 12 Tehni, 12 Guti, and similar regional names). You can play against the CPU or another person on the same device.

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Game settings (sound, haptics, theme) are stored on your device.
  • Current version (v1.0.0): no ads and no in-app purchases. If we enable them later, we will update this policy and Play Store disclosures first.

2. What the App Does

The app lets you:

  • Play the classic 12-bead board game vs CPU or vs another player (hotseat)
  • Adjust sound, haptics, reduced motion, and board themes
  • View in-app help and legal information
  • Optionally remove ads with a one-time Pro purchase (when enabled in a future update)

3. Information We Access

A. Information stored on your device (local only)

  • Preferences such as mute, haptics, reduced motion, and board theme
  • Pro / remove-ads status (when in-app purchases are enabled)

This data stays on your device unless you clear app data or uninstall. We do not operate a cloud account or sync service.

B. Device sensors

The app may read the device accelerometer to add a subtle board tilt effect during gameplay. Sensor data is processed on your device in real time and is not sent to our servers.

C. Internet access

The app may use the internet to:

  • Download typography (Google Fonts) for on-screen text

Current version (v1.0.0): we do not show advertisements and we do not offer in-app purchases. The game works fully offline after fonts are cached.

D. Advertising (AdMob) — not active in v1.0.0

AdMob is not enabled in the current release. If we turn ads on later, AdMob may use advertising identifiers according to Google's policies, and we will update this Privacy Policy and the Play Store Ads / Data safety forms before that release.

E. In-app purchases — not active in v1.0.0

In-app purchases are not enabled in the current release. If we offer a Pro / remove-ads purchase later, transactions will be processed by Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Google, not by us.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Name, email, or phone number (unless you email us for support voluntarily)
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts, SMS, call logs, or calendar
  • Photos, camera, or microphone
  • Passwords or account credentials (there is no app login)

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play — app distribution
  • Google Fonts — typography (may download over HTTPS)

Not active in v1.0.0: Google AdMob advertising and Google Play Billing purchases. If we enable them later, this section and Play Store forms will be updated first.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is a casual board game suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local settings remain until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to respond.

To remove all app data: Android Settings → Apps → Bead 12 → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures such as HTTPS for network requests. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Your Rights and Choices

  • Turn off haptics or reduced motion in Settings
  • Clear app data or uninstall at any time
  • Contact us with privacy questions

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version and update the "Last updated" date. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
