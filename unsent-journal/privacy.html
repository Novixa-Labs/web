Privacy Policy

Unsent Journal: Private Letters

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Novixa-Labs ("we", "us", or "our") operates Unsent Journal: Private Letters (package name: com.novixalabs.unsentjournal). This policy explains what the app accesses, how your words are stored, and your choices.

1. Summary

  • Unsent is a private letter journal for writing letters you do not send.
  • Letters and attachments are stored locally on your device, encrypted with SQLCipher.
  • We do not operate a cloud account for your letter text.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The free version may show banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Unsent Plus is a one-time purchase to remove ads and unlock PDF keepsake exports.
  • Where required, we show Google UMP consent before personalized ads.
  • If you have opened the app and stay away, we may schedule one local reminder per day (via Android notifications). You can disable notifications in system settings.

2. Data on your device

The app stores on your phone:

  • Letter text, moods, rituals progress, and sealed-letter schedules
  • Optional voice note files and image attachments you add
  • App settings (lock, reminders, motion preferences)
  • Encrypted database passphrase in Android Keystore / app-private storage

Backups you create are encrypted files you save yourself (for example via the system file picker). We do not receive them.

3. Permissions

  • Biometric / device lock — optional app lock
  • Microphone — only when you record a voice attachment
  • Notifications — sealed-letter unlock, optional gentle reminders, and idle retention reminder
  • Internet & network state — ads, Play Billing, Firebase, consent, and opening links in your browser
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) — when ads are shown, as allowed by Google Play policies

4. Network & third parties

The app may connect to:

  • Google AdMob — advertising (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Unity Ads — AdMob mediation / no-fill fallback (Unity Privacy Policy)
  • InMobi — AdMob mediation partner (InMobi Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Billing — Unsent Plus purchases
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics — anonymous usage and crash reports (Firebase Privacy)

Letter content is not uploaded to Novixa-Labs servers for journaling features.

5. In-app purchases

Unsent Plus (product ID unsent_journal_pro) is a one-time in-app product processed by Google Play. We receive purchase confirmation to unlock features on your device. Payment details are handled by Google.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices

  • Delete letters or all data inside the app
  • Turn off notifications in Android settings
  • Use Ad privacy choices in Settings when shown
  • Purchase Unsent Plus to remove ads
