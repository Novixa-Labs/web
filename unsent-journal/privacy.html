Privacy Policy

Unsent Journal: Private Letters

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Novixa-Labs ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile application Unsent Journal: Private Letters (package name: com.novixalabs.unsentjournal). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

By installing or using the app, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Summary (Plain Language)

Unsent Journal is a creative private letter journal — a digital space to write letters you do not send, with optional rituals (seal, release, burn) for emotional closure. It is a journaling and creative expression tool, not a game and not medical or therapy care.

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Letters, moods, and attachments are stored on your device, encrypted with SQLCipher.
  • We do not upload your letter text to Novixa-Labs servers for journaling features.
  • The free version may show ads (banner, full-screen interstitial, and optional rewarded ads). You can buy a one-time Unsent Plus purchase (product ID unsent_journal_pro) to remove ads and unlock PDF keepsake exports permanently on your Google account.
  • Where required, we show Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent before personalized ads.
  • We use Google Firebase for anonymous usage analytics and crash reporting to improve the app. This is separate from advertising and we do not sell this data.
  • If you have not opened the app for a while, we may send at most one gentle local notification per day encouraging you to return (you can turn off notifications in Android settings).

2. What the App Does

The app provides:

  • Write and save private unsent letters locally
  • Optional moods, journey tracking, and ritual endings (seal, release, burn)
  • Optional voice notes and image attachments you choose to add
  • Optional app lock (PIN / biometric)
  • Encrypted local backup and export files you save yourself
  • PDF keepsake export (Unsent Plus or optional rewarded unlock for a single export)
  • Optional gentle writing reminders and idle check-in notifications
  • Optional advertising and optional one-time in-app purchase for Unsent Plus
  • Anonymous usage analytics and crash reporting (Firebase) to improve stability and features

3. Information We Access

A. Information stored on your device (local only)

The app saves data on your phone using encrypted local storage, for example:

  • Letter text, titles, recipients, moods, and ritual state
  • Voice note and image attachment files you add
  • App preferences (lock, reminders, motion, notification privacy)
  • Whether you purchased Unsent Plus (premium entitlement flag)
  • Database encryption keys protected with Android Keystore where supported

This data stays on your device unless you export a backup, share content yourself, uninstall the app, or clear app data. We do not operate a cloud sync account for your letters.

B. Photos, audio, and media

When you add an image, the app uses the Android system photo picker or document APIs so you choose what to attach. We do not request broad gallery access to scan your library in the background.

When you record a voice note, the app uses the microphone only while you are recording. Audio is stored locally as part of your letter attachments.

Media you attach is processed locally on your device. We do not upload your letters, voice notes, or images to our servers.

C. Internet access

The app uses the internet to:

  • Load and display advertisements (AdMob and mediation partners)
  • Show consent and ad privacy choices (Google UMP)
  • Process in-app purchases through Google Play Billing
  • Send anonymous analytics events and crash reports to Firebase (Google)
  • Open our privacy policy and support links in your browser when you choose

D. Analytics and crash reporting (Firebase)

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the app is used and to diagnose crashes. These services are provided by Google and are not used to sell your personal information.

Firebase may collect or receive:

  • App version, device model, and Android OS version
  • Screen views, session duration, and general usage events (for example: letter saved, ritual completed, Unsent Plus unlocked)
  • Crash logs, error messages, and stack traces when the app fails
  • An app instance identifier (we do not receive your name, email, or contacts from Firebase for this purpose)

We use this information only to measure performance, understand which features are used, and fix bugs. Firebase may process data on servers operated by Google. See Firebase Privacy and Google Privacy Policy.

If AdMob and Firebase are linked in Google's developer consoles, Google may combine certain analytics signals for ad measurement on the developer side. Ad personalization is still controlled by your Android ad settings, UMP choices, and by purchasing Unsent Plus.

4. Advertising & Advertising ID

If you have not purchased Unsent Plus, the app may display advertisements through Google AdMob and AdMob Mediation partners. Formats include banner ads, full-screen interstitial ads (for example after certain rituals), and optional rewarded video ads (for example to unlock a single PDF export). Mediation may include certified networks such as InMobi, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, and Liftoff (Vungle), depending on availability in your region. The app may also use a Unity Ads client fallback when mediation does not fill an ad request.

The app declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission so ad partners can access the Android Advertising ID (GAID) where permitted by Google Play policy and your device settings.

These services may collect, receive, or share with their partners:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / GAID) and device identifiers permitted by Google Play
  • IP address, device type, OS version, language, and ad interaction data (views, clicks, impressions)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address for ad targeting and measurement
  • Diagnostic and performance data related to ad delivery

This data is used to deliver, measure, personalize (where allowed), and limit ads, and to prevent fraud. We do not receive your letter content, voice notes, or imported photos from ad networks for advertising purposes.

Location: We do not access your device's GPS or precise location for app features. Some ad SDKs (for example InMobi) may declare a location permission in their library manifest; we remove those permissions from the published app because we do not use precise location. Ad partners may still infer approximate location from your IP address to serve and measure ads, consistent with Google Play Data safety disclosures.

You can limit ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads, use Ad privacy choices in the app when offered, or purchase Unsent Plus to stop ad display entirely.

For more information, see Google's ad policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. In-App Purchases (Unsent Plus)

We offer a one-time, non-consumable in-app product (Unsent Plus, product ID unsent_journal_pro) processed by Google Play Billing. This is not a subscription. Google processes payment; we do not store your credit card or bank details.

When you purchase Unsent Plus:

  • Banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads are disabled on that Google account
  • PDF keepsake exports are unlocked without watching rewarded ads

Google may share with us:

  • Purchase confirmation and product ID (to unlock Plus features on your device)
  • Anonymous transaction tokens required to restore purchases

Purchase history is managed by Google Play. Use Restore purchase in the app if you reinstall on the same Google account. Free users may still unlock a single export by watching a rewarded ad instead of buying Plus.

6. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or require:

  • Name, email, or phone number (unless you email us for support voluntarily)
  • Precise location (GPS) from the app
  • Contact list, SMS, call logs, or calendar
  • Broad access to your photo library (only media you explicitly attach)
  • Your letter content for cloud journaling (unless you choose to share or back up files yourself)

We do not operate user accounts or social login in this app.

This app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error reporting (Firebase Privacy)
  • Google AdMob — advertising (Google Privacy Policy)
  • AdMob Mediation partners — InMobi, Unity, Meta, AppLovin, Liftoff (each has its own privacy policy)

8. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Letters and preferences remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to respond to your request.
  • Ad partners retain data according to their own policies and your device settings.
  • Analytics and crash data in Firebase is retained according to Google's default retention periods and is not linked to your identity by us.

To remove all app data: Android Settings → Apps → Unsent Journal → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. You can also delete all data from in-app Privacy & security settings.

10. Security

Letter data is encrypted on device with SQLCipher. We use reasonable measures for HTTPS when the app communicates with ad, billing, and analytics services. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; you control what you write, attach, export, and share.

  • Disable notifications in Android settings
  • Delete letters or all data inside the app
  • Clear app data or uninstall at any time
  • Contact us to ask questions about this policy

12. International Users

The app is offered globally. Data processed on your device is controlled by you on your device. Ad and payment partners may process data in various countries according to their policies.
