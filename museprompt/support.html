2. What MusePrompt Does (and Does Not Do)

  • Browse categories and preview images
  • Assemble AI photo prompts by tapping options
  • Save, copy, and reopen prompts on your device
  • Open external AI tools so you can paste your prompt there

MusePrompt does not generate images itself and does not require an account. Image creation happens only in the external tools you choose to open.

3. Ads & Pro / Remove Ads

  • The free version may show ads (Google AdMob). Where required, a consent form (Google UMP) may appear.
  • Buy the one-time product museprompt_pro (Pro / Remove Ads) to disable ads.
  • If ads return after reinstall, use restore purchases with the same Google account that bought Pro.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under Google's refund policy.

4. Saved Prompts & Data Deletion

Prompts, folders, history, and settings are stored locally (SQLite and preferences). There is no cloud account and therefore no separate account-deletion portal.

To delete all MusePrompt data on your device:

  • Android Settings → Apps → MusePrompt → Storage → Clear data, or
  • Uninstall the app

Clearing data or uninstalling removes local library customizations, saved prompts, and preferences for this app. Google Play purchase records remain with Google and can restore Pro after reinstall on the same account.

5. External Tools

"Open in" actions launch third-party websites or apps (for example Gemini, ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, or Stable Diffusion). Those services are independent. If a tool fails to open, check that you have a browser or the relevant app installed and that your device allows opening links.
