Privacy Policy

Orchard Pop: Fruit Merge

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Novixa-Labs operates Orchard Pop: Fruit Merge (package com.orchardpop.game). The game is a single-player fruit drop and merge puzzle. You do not need an account.

1. Data stored on your device

  • Best scores, discovered fruits, sound and haptics settings, and an in-progress run.
  • Lives count and the timer for the next life.
  • Whether Orchard Plus has been purchased on this Google account (via Google Play Billing).

This data stays on the device except where Google Play, advertising, or Firebase services are involved below. We do not sell personal information.

2. Advertising and consent

The free version shows a banner during play and a limited number of full-screen ads (at most a few per session) when a run starts, when you leave and come back, and when a run ends. You can choose to watch a rewarded video for an extra life or a booster. Where required, a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform is shown before ads load.

Ad partners (including Google AdMob and mediation partners such as Unity Ads) may receive advertising identifiers, coarse location, app activity, and device identifiers to show and measure ads. Their policies apply to that processing.

Orchard Plus is a one-time in-app purchase that removes ads and gives unlimited lives.

3. Firebase

When configured for a release build, the app uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand crashes and basic feature use. Those reports do not include your scores as a public profile. Collection follows Google’s Firebase settings and can be limited in your device’s ad and diagnostic controls.

4. Purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your card number. Restore purchase uses the same Google account.

5. Children

The game is a casual puzzle. It is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help you remove what we can.
