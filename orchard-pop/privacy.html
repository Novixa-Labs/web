Privacy Policy

Orchard Pop: Fruit Merge

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Novixa-Labs operates Orchard Pop: Fruit Merge (package com.orchardpop.game). The game is a single-player fruit drop and merge puzzle. You do not need an account.

1. Data stored on your device

  • Best scores, discovered fruits, sound and haptics settings, and an in-progress run.
  • How many lives remain for the current UTC day. A new day restores five lives. Lives do not refill on a timer.
  • Whether Orchard Plus has been purchased on this Google account (via Google Play Billing).

This data stays on the device except where Google Play, advertising, or Firebase services are involved below. We do not sell personal information.

2. Advertising and consent

The free version may show a banner only after you first interact with a game. A full-screen ad is never shown on app launch, before a run starts, or merely because the app resumes. It may be shown at the natural break after a completed run when you choose to play again or return home. Full-screen ads are limited to two per app session and wait at least three minutes between displays. You can choose to watch a rewarded video for an extra life or booster. Where required, Google’s User Messaging Platform asks for consent before ads load and the game provides access to ad privacy choices.

Ads are served through Google AdMob mediation. Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) may participate in the AdMob auction; Unity may also provide a fallback when an AdMob request has no fill. InMobi is not included. These partners may receive the Android advertising ID, IP address, coarse location, device and app information, consent signals, and ad interaction data to fill, measure, secure, and limit ads. Orchard Plus removes these ads.

Their notices: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses data from sites or apps that use their services, AdMob & AdSense program policies, Unity Privacy Policy, and Liftoff Privacy Policy.

Orchard Plus is a one-time in-app purchase that removes ads and gives unlimited lives.

3. Firebase

Release builds use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google. Analytics records campaign attribution and a limited set of successful gameplay outcomes: a run starting, the first successful drop on an install, a completed-run summary (score and bounded gameplay counts), earned ad rewards, purchases, and actual ad displays. It does not record every tap, movement, physics update, or merge. Crashlytics receives crash diagnostics. These reports are not a public profile of your scores. You can limit ad and diagnostic collection in your device settings. See Firebase privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your card number. Restore purchase uses the same Google account.

5. Children

The game is a casual puzzle. It is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help you remove what we can.
