Full description

Welcome to Marble Workshop, a colorful sorting puzzle where every move keeps the workshop running.

Release glossy marbles onto the conveyor, guide matching colors into their boxes, and pack every order before the belt fills. The rules are easy to learn, but each new workshop layout asks you to plan further ahead.

MASTER THE MARBLE WORKSHOP

- Match three same-color marbles to complete a box
- Reverse the conveyor when the flow works against you
- Park marbles in a limited pocket and release them at the right moment
- Undo a move or use a hint when a difficult setup needs a fresh approach
- Solve deterministic puzzles designed to remain completable

500 PROGRESSIVE PUZZLES

Begin with clear color-matching challenges, then learn new mechanics as the workshop expands. Frozen boxes, linked crates, timed switches and tighter pocket limits add new decisions without changing the simple controls. Milestone levels mark your progress every ten puzzles.

ENDLESS WORKSHOP

Finish the main trail and continue with seed-based challenges and rotating modifiers. Each endless puzzle creates a new workshop setup to solve.

POLISHED AND SATISFYING

Enjoy shiny marbles, smooth conveyor motion, responsive effects and clear color symbols. Play at your own pace with sound, vibration and accessibility options available in Settings.

PLAY OFFLINE

The complete puzzle trail and Endless Workshop can be played without an internet connection. Internet access is used for optional advertising, purchase verification, consent choices, analytics and crash reporting.

Marble Workshop is free to play and contains ads. Optional rewarded videos can provide a rescue when available. Marble Workshop Pro is a one-time Google Play purchase that removes banner and interstitial ads and starts each level with three undos.
