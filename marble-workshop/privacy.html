Marble Workshop Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Developer: Novixa-Labs · Package: com.marbleworkshop.game

1. Overview

Marble Workshop is a single-player puzzle game. It does not require an account and does not ask for your name, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, or camera. Game progress, settings, coins, stars, and purchase entitlement cache are stored locally on your device.

2. Services and information processed

The free version may display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob mediation/open bidding may use Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize (Vungle). If an eligible AdMob request has no fill, Unity Ads may be used as a direct fallback. Depending on consent, region, device settings, and provider configuration, these providers may process advertising or app-instance identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device and app information, ad views/clicks, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals.

Where required, the app uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent and provide privacy choices. An available privacy-options entry appears in Settings. Purchasing Marble Workshop Pro removes banner and interstitial ads; optional rewarded availability may differ by product behavior and region.

3. Analytics and crash reports

Release builds use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics is limited to campaign attribution and meaningful outcomes such as tutorial completion, level starts/completions/failures, milestone progress, rewarded rescues, purchases, and actual ad displays. We do not intentionally log every tap, conveyor update, marble movement, or puzzle solution. Crashlytics may receive crash stack traces, app version, device model, OS version, memory state, and related diagnostics so we can improve reliability.

4. Purchases

Marble Workshop Pro is a one-time in-app product handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes payment details; Novixa-Labs receives purchase status and transaction-related information needed to unlock or restore the product, prevent fraud, and provide support. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

5. Purposes and legal bases

Information is processed to operate the game, save progress, deliver and measure ads, honor purchases, prevent fraud, obtain required consent, understand aggregate engagement, diagnose crashes, and improve the app. Depending on your location, processing relies on consent, performance of the requested service, legitimate interests in security and product improvement, and legal obligations. You may withdraw advertising consent through the in-app privacy options when available or through device settings.

6. Sharing and international processing

We do not sell personal information ourselves. Data may be processed by Google (AdMob, UMP, Firebase, Google Play), Unity, Liftoff Monetize/Vungle, and other Google-certified demand partners configured in AdMob. These providers may act as processors or independent controllers and may process information outside your country under their own safeguards and policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy
  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • Liftoff Privacy Policy

7. Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Advertising, analytics, crash, and purchase records are retained under the relevant provider's settings, legal requirements, and policies. Because the game has no Novixa-Labs account, there is no server account to delete. You may contact us for a privacy request; we may need limited information to locate records, and some provider-held data must be managed through that provider or your Google account.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate. The Play Console target-audience declaration and advertising configuration will be kept consistent with the released app.

9. Security, choices, and changes

We use platform and provider safeguards appropriate to the information processed, but no system is completely secure. You can limit ad personalization in Android/Google settings, use in-app ad privacy choices when shown, decline optional rewarded ads, clear local app data, or uninstall. We may update this policy when features, providers, or laws change; the effective date above identifies the current version.

10. Contact

For privacy, deletion, purchase, or support requests, email bibi85259@gmail.com. Include your app version and country, but do not send passwords, payment-card details, or sensitive files.
