Marble Workshop Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Marble Workshop is provided by Novixa-Labs. The game does not require an account. Game progress and preferences are stored on your device.

Advertising and purchases

The app may show ads supplied by Google AdMob and its mediation partners, and offers an optional one-time purchase through Google Play. These providers may process device identifiers, advertising data, approximate location derived from network information, app interactions, diagnostics, and purchase information to deliver ads, prevent fraud, process purchases, and measure performance. Where required, the app asks for advertising consent and provides access to privacy choices in Settings.

Analytics and crash reports

Firebase Analytics records a limited set of gameplay outcomes such as tutorial completion, level starts and completions, and rewarded rescues. Firebase Crashlytics receives crash and diagnostic information. We use this information to improve stability, understand aggregate engagement, and measure campaigns. We do not record the contents of other apps, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Data retention and control

Local progress can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. Advertising privacy choices are available from the app's Settings where required. Purchase records are maintained by Google Play under Google's policies.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate.
