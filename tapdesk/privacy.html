Privacy Policy

TapDesk: Phone Remote Control

Last updated: September 28, 2026 · Novixa-Labs · package com.novixalabs.tapdesk

TapDesk lets you view and control your own Android phone from a browser, after you start the session and approve the permissions. It is a remote-help tool for a phone you own or a phone whose owner is with you and has agreed. It is not a monitoring, tracking, or hidden-control product.

1. What you control

  • The launcher icon stays visible. TapDesk does not hide itself.
  • A remote session starts only after you open it in the app and enter the pairing PIN on the computer.
  • You can stop a session in the app, turn off Accessibility, stop screen capture, or uninstall at any time.
  • Same Wi-Fi does not ask you to sign in.
  • Secure Link’s one free session is remembered on the Google account you choose, so reinstalling the app does not grant that free session again. We store an account id for that purpose. We do not fingerprint the phone.

2. Information used to run a session

  • Screen — shown in the browser only after you approve Android’s screen-capture prompt. A notification stays visible while sharing is on. TapDesk does not start screen capture by itself in the background, and Novixa-Labs does not store a history of your screen. The app does not use the broad “see all installed apps” permission. It can show launcher apps so you can open one during a session.
  • Accessibility — not an accessibility tool for disabilities. Before you can turn it on, TapDesk shows a disclosure. You then enable the service yourself in Android settings. It performs taps, swipes, navigation, and text input requested from a browser session you paired. It does not decide or act on its own, and it does not bypass Android security screens on its own.
  • Pairing PIN and room code — generated on the phone so the computer can join. Treat them as secrets.
  • Same Wi-Fi — traffic stays between the phone and the computer on your local network. The desk page is loaded from our website and then served by the phone so the browser can reach it.
  • Another network (Secure Link) — the phone and browser exchange the session, including the screen for that session, through a connection service. When a direct path is possible, video prefers a peer-to-peer WebRTC link. A TURN relay is used only when a direct path is not possible. Novixa-Labs does not keep a recording of your screen.

3. Ads and one-time purchases

Same Wi-Fi connects the browser directly on your network. Starting that session can use an optional video you choose to watch, or Remove Ads / Premium. It does not require a Google account. Ads, when shown, appear on Home and Settings while the app is open, including during a session. A full-screen ad is not shown when the app opens or resumes. It can appear after you tap Back or Stop, once a browser has connected, and not in the same moment as a rewarded video.

Secure Link is the path for a computer that is not on the same Wi-Fi. The first successful Secure Link session is free on the Google account you sign in with. After that, you can watch an optional video for one more session, a few times a day. Watching is your choice. A failed connection does not use up the free session.

These are one-time Google Play products, not subscriptions, and they stay on your Google account:

  • Remove Ads (tapdesk_remove_ads, $2.99) — removes banners and full-screen ads. It does not include Secure Link.
  • Secure Link (tapdesk_secure_link, $4.99) — unlimited Secure Link. It does not remove ads.
  • TapDesk Premium (tapdesk_premium, $6.99) — Secure Link and Remove Ads together.

Purchases are processed by Google. Where required, we show Google’s consent form before requesting personalized ads. Advertising partners may include Google AdMob and, as a fallback, Unity Ads.

4. Analytics and crashes

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand feature use and fix crashes. This is separate from advertising. We do not sell this data. You can limit ad personalization in your device settings and, where offered, in Ad privacy inside the app.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use the app to secretly watch another person.
  • We do not direct TapDesk at children under 13.
