Privacy Policy

TapDesk: Phone Remote Control

Last updated: October 4, 2026 · Novixa-Labs · Android package com.novixalabs.tapdesk

Novixa-Labs ("we", "us", or "our") operates TapDesk, a remote-assistance app that lets you view and control your own Android phone from a web browser after you start a session and approve the permissions. TapDesk is not a monitoring, stalkerware, or hidden-control product. The app icon stays visible; nothing runs as a secret background spy.

By installing or using TapDesk, you agree to this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

1. Summary (plain language)

  • You start every remote session and can stop it anytime from the phone.
  • Screen content is sent only to the browser session you paired — we do not store a recording of your screen on our servers.
  • Accessibility is used only for remote control you request from that browser session; TapDesk is not an accessibility tool for disabilities.
  • Same Wi‑Fi does not require a Google account. Traffic stays on your local network except loading the desk page from our website.
  • Secure Link (another network) may use Google sign-in for a one-time free session per account, plus encrypted relay services described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Free use may include ads (Google AdMob and partners). One-time Play purchases can remove ads and/or unlock Secure Link.
  • We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to improve stability — separate from selling data.

2. What TapDesk does

  • Live screen view in a browser (after Android screen-capture approval)
  • Remote taps, swipes, navigation, and typing via Accessibility (after in-app disclosure and your manual enable in system settings)
  • File transfer from browser to phone (saved to your Downloads)
  • Same Wi‑Fi pairing (QR / link + PIN) and Secure Link pairing (room code + PIN)
  • Optional on-device Network & Tools utilities
  • Optional advertising and Google Play one-time purchases

3. Information stored on your device (local)

TapDesk keeps data on the phone using Android local storage, for example:

  • Pairing PIN, room codes, and session tokens for active connections
  • Whether you accepted the in-app Accessibility and screen-sharing disclosures
  • Screen-capture consent state (MediaProjection grant managed by Android)
  • Session history metadata (time, mode — not a copy of your screen)
  • Names and timestamps of files received from a browser into Downloads
  • Purchase flags (ads removed, Secure Link unlocked) tied to Google Play on this device
  • Theme and optional daily network reminder preference
  • A random install identifier used only for local session bookkeeping (not sold to advertisers)

Uninstalling the app or clearing app data removes this local information.

4. Screen sharing, foreground service, and notifications

When you approve Android's screen capture prompt, TapDesk mirrors the display to your paired browser for that session. A persistent notification stays visible while the agent is running and while screen sharing is active, as required by Android. TapDesk uses a foreground service (including types media projection and special use) so remote assistance you started is not killed silently in the background. We do not start screen capture without your action.

Novixa-Labs does not operate a cloud DVR of your screen. We do not upload your screen to our servers for storage.

5. Accessibility API

TapDesk uses Android's AccessibilityService to perform gestures and input you request from an authenticated browser session (taps, swipes, Back, Home, text entry, and similar). Before you enable the service, TapDesk shows a prominent in-app disclosure. You must turn the service on yourself in Android Settings. You can turn it off at any time.

  • TapDesk is not designed as a general accessibility aid for people with disabilities.
  • The service does not act on its own or monitor the phone for a third party without a session you approved.
  • Remote control commands are accepted only while a browser is paired with your PIN or password shown in TapDesk.

6. Same Wi‑Fi sessions

  • The browser loads a desk page from novixa-labs.vercel.app, then connects to the phone over your local Wi‑Fi (HTTP/WebSocket on the phone).
  • Screen frames and control data for that mode stay on your LAN except for the initial page load and any ads/analytics on our site.
  • No Google account is required for Same Wi‑Fi.

7. Secure Link (another network)

When phone and computer are not on the same network, TapDesk can use Secure Link:

  • Signaling and video fallback — Session messages and, when used, compressed screen frames may pass through a TLS-encrypted MQTT message relay (HiveMQ Cloud). Room topics are scoped to your pairing code. This path is for live session delivery, not long-term storage.
  • WebRTC — Control uses an encrypted WebRTC data channel when available. Optional HD screen video may use peer-to-peer WebRTC; if direct connection fails, a TURN relay (Metered.ca) may carry encrypted media. We do not record those streams.
  • Connect page — The browser opens our hosted Connect page over HTTPS to enter room code and PIN.
  • Google sign-in (optional) — The first free Secure Link session per Google account uses Google Sign-In. We send a Google ID token to our entitlement API (novixa-labs.vercel.app/api/tapdesk-entitlement) to record trial use against that account (so reinstalling the app does not reset the free trial). We store a session token on the device, not your Google password.

Secure Link is optional. Same Wi‑Fi does not use this account service.

8. Network & Tools

On-device utilities (no account required):

  • Local network details — IP, gateway, subnet, DNS, Wi‑Fi band/signal via Android APIs. TapDesk does not request location permission and does not read Wi‑Fi SSID.
  • Public IP — optional lookup via api.ipify.org when you open the screen.
  • Speed test — when you tap Run, test traffic to Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com).
  • Reachability — tests a host/port you enter.
  • Device details — shown on device only; not uploaded.
  • Received files — saved to Downloads; a short local list of names/times on the phone.
  • Optional daily network reminder — off by default; at most one notification per day if you enable it in Settings; no ads in that notification.

9. Ads, advertising ID, and consent

If you have not purchased Remove Ads or TapDesk Premium, TapDesk may show banner ads on Home and Settings while those screens are open. A full-screen ad may appear after you end a session (for example after Back or Stop), not as a surprise on cold start. Rewarded videos are optional ways to start certain sessions — you choose whether to watch.

Ads are served through Google AdMob and mediation partners (which may include Unity Ads and InMobi, depending on region). These services may process the Android Advertising ID, IP address, device type, language, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads. Where required, we show Google's consent form before personalized ads. You can manage choices in Settings → Ad privacy when available, or limit ad personalization in Android system settings, or reset the advertising ID.

10. In-app purchases (Google Play)

One-time products (not subscriptions), processed by Google Play — we do not store card numbers:

  • Remove Ads (tapdesk_remove_ads)
  • Secure Link (tapdesk_secure_link)
  • TapDesk Premium (tapdesk_premium) — includes Remove Ads and Secure Link

Google shares purchase confirmation so the app can unlock features on your account/device. Use Restore purchases after reinstall on the same Google account.

11. Analytics and crash reporting

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand feature use and fix crashes. This may include app version, device model, OS version, coarse usage events, and crash stack traces. This is not sold as personal information. See Firebase Privacy and Google Privacy Policy.

12. What we do not collect

  • We do not require your name, email, or phone number to use Same Wi‑Fi (unless you email us for support).
  • We do not request GPS or precise location.
  • We do not read SMS, call logs, contacts, or your photo gallery for remote control.
  • We do not use TapDesk to spy on someone without their knowledge — misuse violates our Terms and may violate law.
