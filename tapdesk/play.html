Title

TapDesk: Remote Phone

Short description

Control your Android from a computer. Same Wi-Fi or anywhere you approve.

Full description

TapDesk puts your Android phone on the computer in front of you.

Help your own phone, or sit with a friend and fix theirs, from a normal browser. You start the session. You see the pairing PIN. You can stop it at any time. The TapDesk icon stays on the home screen.

SAME WI-FI
Phone and computer on one network. Open the link TapDesk shows, enter the 6-digit PIN, and work on the live screen: tap, swipe, Back, Home, and open apps.

ANY NETWORK
Start a room on the phone. On the computer, open the TapDesk Connect page, enter the room code and PIN, and continue — even when you are not on the same Wi-Fi.

YOU STAY IN CHARGE
• Accessibility is used only to send the taps you request during a session you started
• Screen sharing uses Android’s capture permission and a visible notification
• Stop a session from the phone whenever you want
• No account required

FREE, WITH A CLEAR UPGRADE
Watch a short video to open one session, on this network or another. Or get TapDesk Pro once on your Google account: unlimited sessions and no ads. Not a subscription.

TapDesk is for help you intend to give. It is not a hidden monitoring tool.
