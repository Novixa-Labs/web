📱 REMOTE CONTROL FOR YOUR ANDROID PHONE TapDesk turns any web browser into a full remote control for your Android phone — no desktop app, no drivers, no cable. View your phone screen live, tap, swipe, type, and manage your device from a computer, laptop, or Chromebook in seconds. 🌐 REMOTE DESKTOP FOR ANDROID ✨ Live screen mirroring — see your phone screen on your computer in real time 🖱️ Tap, swipe, and scroll your Android from a mouse or trackpad ⌨️ Type on your phone using your computer keyboard 📂 Transfer files between your phone and your browser 🔐 Every session is pin-protected — nothing connects without your code 📷 Scan a QR code to connect instantly on the same Wi‑Fi 🌍 Connect from anywhere with a simple room code — no IP address needed ⚡ End any session instantly, right from the phone 🧰 BUILT-IN NETWORK & WIFI TOOLS TapDesk is also a genuinely useful network toolkit that lives on your phone: 📡 IP address finder — local IP, public IP, gateway, subnet, and DNS 📶 WiFi analyzer — band, frequency, link speed, and signal strength ⚡ Internet speed test — one tap for download speed, upload speed, ping, and connection stability (jitter) 🔎 Connection check — see if a computer or device answers on your network, great for troubleshooting 📱 Device info — model, Android version, memory, storage, uptime 🔒 PRIVATE, SESSION-BASED, AND UNDER YOUR CONTROL TapDesk is a remote-assistance tool built entirely around sessions you start and approve. • Every connection is protected by a pairing code • Stop a session instantly from the phone — control ends immediately • End-to-end encrypted control channel for browser sessions • No hidden background access — nothing runs unless you open the app • Screen sharing starts only after you approve Android’s own prompt • The app icon always stays visible — nothing runs hidden 🛡️ PERMISSIONS, EXPLAINED PLAINLY • Screen capture shows your Android screen in the browser only after you accept Android’s screen-share prompt, with a notification visible the whole time. • Accessibility service performs the taps, swipes, and typing you request from your own paired browser session — never on its own, and never in the background. • A foreground-service notification keeps your session visible and under your control while it runs. TapDesk only runs when you open it. It never starts itself on boot and never runs unattended in the background. 💻 USE CASES 📞 Remote tech support — help someone troubleshoot their Android phone 🖥️ Mirror your phone to a bigger screen for demos or presentations 🎓 Show an app or walk through steps from your computer 📱 Manage your own phone remotely without picking it up 🛠️ Test and QA an Android app straight from your browser 🚀 NO DESKTOP CLIENT REQUIRED Just a browser — Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari all work.
