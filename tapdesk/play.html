📱 Control Your Android From Your Browser TapDesk turns your web browser into a remote control for your Android phone. View your phone screen, tap, swipe, type, and interact with your device from a computer — without installing a desktop client. 🌐 REMOTE ANDROID FROM YOUR BROWSER Use your laptop, desktop, or Chromebook to access your Android phone through a browser. ✨ View your phone screen 🖱️ Tap, swipe, and scroll remotely ⌨️ Type and control your phone from your computer 📂 Transfer supported files 🔐 Pair each session with a PIN ⚡ Disconnect whenever you want 🏠 FREE ON THE SAME WI-FI When your phone and computer are on the same network, TapDesk gives you direct browser access. No video and no purchase. Start TapDesk, open the address it shows, and enter the pairing PIN. 🌍 SECURE LINK ON OTHER NETWORKS Need the computer and the phone on different networks? Start a Secure Link session, open the TapDesk Connect page, and pair with the room code and PIN. One short video covers that session. TapDesk Pro removes ads and includes Secure Link without a video each time. One payment on your Google account — not a subscription. Same Wi-Fi stays free either way. 🔒 PRIVATE AND SESSION-BASED TapDesk is built around sessions you start. • Pairing PIN protection • Stop the session from the phone • Encrypted connection when you are not on the same Wi-Fi • No desktop software • Your screen is shared only for the active session • The TapDesk icon stays visible 🛡️ PERMISSIONS YOU APPROVE • Screen capture — shows your Android screen in the browser only after you accept Android’s prompt. A notification stays visible while sharing is on. TapDesk does not start screen capture by itself in the background. • Accessibility — performs taps, swipes, navigation, and text input that you request from a paired browser. TapDesk shows a disclosure first. You turn the service on yourself. TapDesk does not use it to decide or act on its own, and it is not an accessibility tool for disabilities. • Foreground service — keeps a session you started running, with a visible notification • Battery — helps an approved session stay connected 💻 USE CASES 📞 Help someone troubleshoot their Android phone 🖥️ Control your phone from your computer 🎓 Demonstrate an Android app 📱 Manage your own device remotely 🛠️ Provide remote technical assistance 📺 Show your Android screen on a larger display 🚀 NO DESKTOP CLIENT TapDesk works through a supported web browser.
