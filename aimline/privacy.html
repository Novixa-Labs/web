Privacy Policy

Aimline: FPS Aim Trainer

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Novixa-Labs operates Aimline: FPS Aim Trainer (package com.aimline.crosshairstudio on Google Play). It is a single-player offline aim-training game in landscape. You do not need an account. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Data stored on your device

  • Crosshair configuration, saved presets, sound, haptics, particle settings, and whether you enabled practice reminders.
  • Session statistics such as best score, accuracy, and session count.
  • How many training lives and rewarded opportunities remain for the current UTC day.
  • Whether Aimline Plus has been purchased on this Google account through Google Play Billing.

This data stays on your device except where Google Play, advertising, Firebase, or notification scheduling involves Google or Android system services as described below.

2. Advertising and consent

The free version can show a banner in a reserved area outside gameplay controls. A full-screen ad may appear only after a completed training round, subject to availability, consent, a minimum two-minute cooldown, and a maximum of two per app session. Closing or opening the app does not itself show a full-screen ad. You may choose to watch a rewarded video for an extra life or round when that option is presented.

Where required by law or platform rules, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) collects advertising consent before personalized ads load. You can change consent later in the app or device settings when available.

Ads are requested from Google AdMob first, with Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) as bidding partners. If an eligible request has no fill, Unity Ads may show directly as a fallback. These partners may receive the Android advertising ID, IP address, coarse location, device and app information, and ad interaction data to deliver, measure, and limit ads. Aimline Plus is a one-time purchase that removes banner and full-screen ads.

Partner and program notices: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses data from sites or apps that use their services, AdMob & AdSense program policies, Unity Privacy Policy, and Liftoff Privacy Policy.

3. Firebase

Release builds use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, to understand broad app use and receive crash reports. Analytics records bounded events such as tutorial completion, practice starts and completed rounds; it does not record individual shots or frame-by-frame gameplay. Crash reports help fix stability issues. See Firebase privacy information.

4. Optional local reminders

Practice reminders are off by default. If you turn them on and grant Android notification permission where required, the app schedules one local reminder about a day after you last opened Aimline. Opening the app resets that pending reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your device; we do not operate a marketing mailing list from it. You can turn reminders off in the app or system notification settings at any time.

5. Purchases

Payments for Aimline Plus are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your card number. Restore purchase uses the same Google account.

6. Children

The game is an aim trainer for general audiences. It is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us and we will help within our control.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The Last updated date at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
