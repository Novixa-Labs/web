Privacy Policy

Aimline: Crosshair Trainer

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Novixa-Labs operates Aimline: Crosshair Trainer (package com.aimline.crosshairstudio on Google Play). It is a single-player offline aim-training game in landscape. You do not need an account. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Data stored on your device

  • Crosshair configuration, saved presets (up to twenty), sound, haptics, and particle settings.
  • Session statistics such as best score, accuracy, and session count.
  • How many training lives remain for the current UTC day (five restored each calendar day in UTC).
  • Whether Aimline Plus has been purchased on this Google account (via Google Play Billing).

This data stays on your device except where Google Play, advertising, Firebase, or notification scheduling involves Google or Android system services as described below.

2. Advertising and consent

The free version shows a banner at the top during a mission, away from the crosshair and fire control. A full-screen ad may appear when you start or finish a training run. A session shows at most three full-screen ads, and a later one waits at least two minutes after the previous successful action. Closing the app and reopening it does not by itself show an ad. You may choose to watch a rewarded video for one extra life when you are out of lives.

Where required by law or platform rules, Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) collects your advertising consent before personalized ads load. You can change consent later in the app or device settings when available.

Ads are requested from Google AdMob first (Open Bidding with Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) as bidding partners). If that request has no fill, Unity Ads may show directly as a fallback. These partners may receive the Android advertising ID, IP address, coarse location, device and app information, and ad interaction data to deliver, measure, and limit ads. Aimline Plus (one-time purchase) removes banner and full-screen ads.

Partner and program notices: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses data from sites or apps that use their services, AdMob & AdSense program policies, Unity Privacy Policy, and Liftoff Privacy Policy.

3. Firebase (release builds)

Release builds use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, to understand basic feature use and to receive crash reports. Crash reports help fix stability issues; they are not a public profile of your scores. You can limit ad and diagnostic collection in your device settings. See Firebase privacy information.

4. Optional local reminders

If you allow notifications on Android 13+, the app may schedule a local reminder about one day after you last opened Aimline (for example, an evening nudge to practice). The reminder is scheduled on your device; we do not operate a marketing mailing list from it. You can turn notifications off in system settings at any time.

5. Purchases

Payments for Aimline Plus are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your card number. Restore purchase uses the same Google account.

6. Children

The game is an aim trainer for general audiences. It is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us and we will help within our control.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
