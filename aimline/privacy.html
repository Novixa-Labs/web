Privacy Policy

Aimline: Crosshair Trainer

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Novixa-Labs operates Aimline: Crosshair Trainer (package com.aimline.crosshairstudio). It is a single-player offline aim-training game. No account is required.

1. Data stored on your device

  • Crosshair configuration, presets, sound and haptics settings, and session statistics.
  • How many training lives remain for the current UTC day (five restored each day).
  • Whether Aimline Plus has been purchased on this Google account (Google Play Billing).

This data stays on the device except where Google Play, advertising, or Firebase services are involved below.

2. Advertising and consent

The free version shows a banner during missions. A full-screen ad may appear when you start or finish a run (at most three per app session, with at least two minutes between later full-screen ads). You may watch a rewarded video for an extra life. Where required, Google’s User Messaging Platform collects consent before ads load.

Ads are requested from Google AdMob first (Open Bidding with Unity and Liftoff/Vungle mediation). If AdMob has no fill, Unity Ads may show directly. Partners may receive the advertising ID, IP address, coarse location, device information, and ad interaction data. Aimline Plus removes ads.

See Google Privacy Policy, Google advertising data, Unity Privacy Policy, and Liftoff Privacy Policy.

3. Firebase

Release builds use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to measure basic usage and receive crash reports. See Firebase privacy.

4. Purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your card number.
