Privacy Policy

Y2K Camera · Effective date: November 26, 2026

Novixa-Labs operates Y2K Camera (package com.novixalabs.y2kcamera), a retro camera app. This policy explains the information processed when you use the app. You do not need an account.

Photos and videos

Photos and videos are processed on your device and saved only to your own gallery. We do not upload your photos or videos to Novixa-Labs.

Permissions

The camera permission is used to capture photos and videos. The microphone is used only when you record a video with sound. Importing from your gallery uses Android's system photo picker and does not require a broad media permission. The app does not request a location permission, and location and EXIF GPS data are stripped from exports.

Advertising, consent, analytics, and crashes

The free version may show advertising through Google AdMob, with mediation partners Unity Ads and Liftoff (Vungle). Those services may process the advertising ID, device information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interactions. For users in the EEA and the UK, Google User Messaging Platform requests consent before ads are requested, and Settings includes a Privacy choices option. Buying Pro removes ads.

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, which may process app interactions, crash logs, and device information.

Purchases

Google Play Billing processes the one-time Pro purchase (product ID pro_unlock). Payment is handled by Google. We do not receive your full payment-card number. Google Play and the app process product, purchase-state, and entitlement information to provide and restore the purchase.

Children

Y2K Camera is not directed at children under 13.

Choices, deletion, and contact

Uninstalling the app removes local data stored by the app. For privacy questions, email bibi85259@gmail.com. Please do not send private photos, videos, or payment information by email.

Service providers and changes

Google Play, Google AdMob, Google UMP, Firebase, Unity Ads, and Liftoff (Vungle) process information under their own policies, including the Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information, Unity Privacy Policy, and Liftoff Privacy Policy. We may update this policy when the app or its providers change.
