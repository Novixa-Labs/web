TV Control Support

TV Control is an independent Wi-Fi remote for compatible smart TVs.

Getting connected

  1. Connect your phone and TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open TV Control and allow local-network access when Android asks.
  3. Select a compatible TV and complete the pairing prompt shown on that TV.

Voice search

Voice search starts only after you tap its button. Grant microphone permission if you want to use it. Recognition behavior depends on your device's configured speech-recognition provider.

Remove local data

Use Android Settings → Apps → TV Control → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. This removes locally saved TVs and settings.
