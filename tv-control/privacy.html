Privacy Policy

TV Control: Wi-Fi Remote
Package: com.novixalabs.tvcontrol
Last updated: November 17, 2026

Novixa-Labs operates TV Control, an independent utility that lets you send remote-control commands to compatible smart TVs on the same local Wi-Fi network. This policy explains the information the app accesses and your choices.

Information the app uses

  • Local network data: the app discovers compatible TVs and sends the commands you choose. Paired-TV names, local addresses, preferences, and the certificate material needed for secure pairing are stored locally on your device.
  • Microphone: only when you tap the voice-search control, the app records speech for your connected TV. Recognition is provided by the speech-recognition service configured on your device; depending on that provider and its settings, audio may be processed on-device or by that provider. We do not retain recordings or independently upload raw microphone audio.
  • Notifications: only if you grant permission, the app may send local reminders. You can turn this off in Android settings.

Data we do not request

TV Control does not require an account and does not request contacts, SMS, call logs, photos, files, precise location, or browsing history. We do not sell personal information.

Storage, security, and deletion

Pairing details and settings remain in the app's local storage. Android backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for these preferences. To remove local data, use Android Settings → Apps → TV Control → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

Advertising and consent

The free version may show banner and natural-break interstitial ads through Google AdMob. AdMob mediation may use Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize. A rewarded ad unit is configured for optional, user-initiated reward features; no reward is granted unless an ad completes successfully.

Advertising providers may process the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, ad impressions, interactions, and diagnostics. We do not request precise GPS location for advertising. Where required, Google User Messaging Platform presents consent choices and an in-app privacy-options entry point. Buying Remove Ads stops ad display but does not disable analytics or crash reporting.

Analytics and crash reporting

Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics process app-installation identifiers, app version, device model, operating-system version, bounded usage events, and crash diagnostics. Events are limited to useful outcomes such as connection success, delivered remote use, rewards, purchases, and actual ad impressions; TV names, local IP addresses, pairing credentials, raw audio, and typed remote text are not sent as analytics parameters.

Purchases

TV Control may offer a one-time Remove Ads product through Google Play Billing. Google processes payment and may provide the app with product, purchase-token, and entitlement information needed to unlock or restore the purchase. Novixa-Labs does not receive or store your card or bank details.

Third-party services

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform, Google Play Billing, Unity Ads, and Liftoff Monetize. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy and legal obligations.

Children

TV Control is not directed to children under the minimum age required in their country. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information to us through support.
